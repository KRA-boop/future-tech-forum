B07 | High Forehand Serve in Singles

B07 · Beginner · Approx. 25 minutes

What You'll Learn in This Lesson

The ability to return a high serve to the backcourt in the diagonal service box.

Recommended to Learn First B05 Backhand Drop Shot

Technique and Decision-Making

Hold the racket loosely, maintain a steady grip and consistent rhythm, and hit the shuttlecock high along the diagonal. Start by aiming for a slightly wider landing zone; once you’ve established a consistent swing, gradually adjust the landing point to a narrower area. For official matches, follow the applicable competition rules regarding the serve.

Court Practice

  1. Serve 10 shuttles from each side. Aim for a specific area at the back of the court, and keep track of shots that are too short, go out of bounds, or land in the target area.
  2. Qualification Criteria Land at least 6 shots on each side within the designated area, and be in position before your opponent returns the serve after serving.

Common Mistakes and Corrections

If you’re applying more and more power but the shuttlecock isn’t going high, check your racket face and the direction of your stroke. Start by practicing with a slow swing to aim for the correct landing spot.

Self-check

If one serve goes deep but the next one often goes out of bounds, which aspect should you work on first?

First, establish your stance, grip, and swing rhythm, then gradually adjust the power of your shots.

Advanced Practice

In the second round, have your partner stand on the opposite side as the receiver and observe whether you’re still watching the landing spot after serving. If you haven’t returned to your ready position by the time your opponent hits the shuttlecock, reduce the power of your next serve, aim for a specific landing zone, and then adjust your positioning.
