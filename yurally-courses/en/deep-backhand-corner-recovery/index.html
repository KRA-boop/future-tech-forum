D08 | Returning to Position When Forced to the Backhand Backcourt

D08 · In-Game Application · Approx. 35 minutes

What You'll Learn in This Lesson

Be able to distinguish between two types of defensive shots: those where you can turn in time to return the shuttlecock and those where you are forced to hit the shuttlecock late.

Recommended to Learn First D07 Clear Shots and Returning to Position

Technique and Decision-Making

When you arrive at the net early, use a sideways stance to return controllable shots with the top of your head or your forehand. When you arrive late, choose a line that allows you to consistently return the shuttle deep or short, and first take a step back to cover your opponent’s return. During practice, note whether you arrived early or late for each shot.

Court Practice

  1. Start from the center. Your partner will alternate between hitting backcourt shots that arrive early and those that arrive late—12 shots total. After each shot, check whether you can return to the starting position.
  2. Qualification Criteria Be able to return at least 8 shuttlecocks over the net to a specific landing zone, and explain how to minimize the risk when a shuttlecock arrives late.

Common Mistakes and Corrections

Forcing a cross-court shot can easily throw you off balance. First, identify your most consistent landing spot, then gradually add variety.

Self-check

If You Can Only Return the Shuttlecock to the Middle or Front Court, Do You Have to Run Back to the Geometric Center?

Adjust your position based on the direction of the incoming shot and your opponent’s contact point, and first return to a position that covers the likely trajectory of the next shot.

Advanced Practice

Use side-view footage to compare two shots—one where you arrive early and one where you arrive late—to identify the exact moment when your focus shifts from moving to striking the shuttlecock. In the next round, adjust only your first step and the position of your racket head, and observe whether you can return to your ready position one beat earlier.
