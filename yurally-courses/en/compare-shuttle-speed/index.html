What You'll Learn in This Lesson

Allows for a consistent comparison of the flight depth of two different shuttlecocks on the same court.

Recommended to Learn First G08—How to Choose Between Feather and Nylon Shuttlecocks

Technique and Decision-Making

First, check the shuttlecock for damage, and choose a new shuttlecock or practice shuttlecock in similar condition. Air conditioning, wind direction, temperature, and variations in power can all affect where the shuttlecock lands. Re-test after switching sides of the court to help distinguish between the shuttlecock’s performance and environmental factors. In official matches, use the shuttlecocks specified by the organizers.

Court Practice

  1. Play with the same partner, using the same swing and starting point, and hit 6 comparison shots each. Mark the midpoint of the landing spots, switch directions, and repeat the exercise.
  2. Qualification Criteria Be able to use landing points in two directions to identify differences and point out factors that may affect the results.

Common Mistakes and Corrections

Comparing worn shuttlecocks directly with new ones can lead to confusion about shuttlecock speed and wear. Start by selecting shuttlecocks in similar condition.

Self-check

If the shuttlecock is only observed to travel deeper on one side of the court, can we conclude that that particular shuttlecock is faster?

First, switch sides and retest while controlling the force of your serve; the results are more reliable only when the trends in both directions are consistent.

Advanced Practice

Ask another player to switch the order of the shuttlecocks without looking at the labels to reduce preconceived judgments. Record the fan’s position and the shuttlecock’s wear and tear, then repeat the exercise on the same court after some time to see if the same trend reoccurs.
