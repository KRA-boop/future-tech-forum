B12 | Four-Week Basic Training Schedule

YouTube Tutorial Videos

Watch the original video on YouTube

Key Points to Watch: Observe how the weekly training themes, intensity, and rest periods are structured, and consider how to adjust them based on your own abilities.

After-Class Exercises: Develop a four-week plan, and track your serve success rate, six-point footwork, and depth of your clear shots each week; use these results to adjust the focus for the following week.

B12 · In-Game Application · Approx. 35 minutes

What You'll Learn in This Lesson

The ability to integrate basic movements into a structured training plan.

Recommended to Learn First B11 Forehand and Backhand Net Shots

Technique and Decision-Making

Choose two topics to practice each week. Start each session by practicing a single movement, then move on to two-shot drills with a partner. Repeat the drills using the same shuttle-feeding method, shuttles, and court layout, and record the number of successful hits, the number of times you lost your balance, and how you felt after completing the drill.

Court Practice

  1. Week 1—practice grip and footwork; Week 2—add serves; Week 3—add net play and baseline play; Week 4—play short rallies. Set aside 5 minutes each week to retest the same skill.
  2. Qualification Criteria Compare the same test from Week 1 and Week 4, and select a goal for the next session.

Common Mistakes and Corrections

If you try to learn too many techniques at once, it will be hard to know what to work on. Focus on just one main goal and one review item each time.

Self-check

My score didn't go up in Week 4, but the number of times I lost my balance went down. How should I record this?

Keep the results from these two exercises. Maintaining your balance is a step forward; next time, test your accuracy in hitting the same landing spots under the same conditions.

Advanced Practice

On the last day of Week 4, keep a brief record listing the training dates, number of sessions attended, retest results, and feedback from your practice partner. For the next round, choose only one repeatable test to avoid setting too many goals.
