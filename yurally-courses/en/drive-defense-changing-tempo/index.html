D06 | Varying the Speed of the Flat Defensive Drive

YouTube Tutorial Videos

Watch the original video on YouTube

Key Points to Watch: Observe the short swing, racket face, and follow-through of the backhand flat drive defense to identify the contact points you can control.

After-Class Exercises: In each set, start with three flat drives, then gently block suitable returns to the front court; perform 10 sets, noting the landing points and recovery positions when the pace changes.

D06 · Advanced · Approx. 30 minutes

What You'll Learn in This Lesson

The ability to identify shots in fast rallies that can be used to change the rhythm by blocking them short.

Recommended to Learn First D05 Introduction to the Flat Drive Defense

Technique and Decision-Making

First, make sure your racket is in front of you, then assess the height of the incoming shot. When the height and speed of the shot allow you to control the racket face, you can absorb the power and block it short; if your opponent is already close to the net or the shot is too low, retreat to a safe zone to keep the rally going.

Court Practice

  1. After four straight drives on the half-court, the defender chooses on the fifth shot whether to block short or continue with a straight drive. Perform 10 sets; your partner should record the choice and the actual landing zone.
  2. Qualification Criteria Out of 10 shots, you should be able to control at least 6 of them; after blocking a short shot, follow up with your next shot.

Common Mistakes and Corrections

If you return every shot short, you’ll lose your options. First, establish a steady rhythm, then have the feeder mix in variations in height.

Self-check

When Your Opponent Is Already in the Front Court Waiting for a Drop Shot, How Should You Adjust Your Shot Speed?

First, get back into position to protect yourself, then try a deep flat drive or a high clear to pull your opponent out of position.

Advanced Practice

Finish by playing a two-minute rally with your partner. Whenever an opportunity arises to play a drop shot, make a mental note of it, and then review it together after the rally ends. Compare this to the moments when you actually played a drop shot to see if you missed any safer options.
