A02 | Smash Landing Points and the Second Shot

A02 · Advanced · Approx. 30 minutes

What You'll Learn in This Lesson

The ability to choose where to place the shuttlecock based on defensive positioning, and to be ready to return the next shot immediately after hitting it.

Recommended to Learn First A01 Introduction to the Smash

Technique and Decision-Making

Before making contact with the shuttlecock in the air, use your peripheral vision to check where your opponent is positioned. If your opponent is positioned toward the sideline, test the side landing zone while leaving ample space within the court; if your opponent raises their racket high, use the downward smash as the first step to create a short return. Immediately after hitting the first shot, return to a ready stance, then decide whether to move to the net or return to the center based on the opponent’s response.

Court Practice

  1. Start with 6 shots each of half-speed smashes down the line and to the backhand side. Then do 8 sets of double shots: hit the first shot with a downward angle, and feed the second shot randomly to the front court.
  2. Qualification Criteria Out of 8 sets, complete at least 5 sets of smashes, returns, and second-shuttle returns.

Common Mistakes and Corrections

If you spend too long watching where the smash lands, you’ll end up making the next shot an easy one for your opponent. First, slow down your speed to a level where you can return the shot consistently.

Self-check

When your partner is standing in a straight line with their racket raised in front of them, what are the consequences of simply applying more power to that shot?

Your opponent may use your power to block the shot, and if you hit it too hard, it will be harder to return the second shot. Try aiming for a shot that lands close to your opponent or a more controlled drop shot.

Advanced Practice

In the second round, have the feeder randomly return to the left or right side of the net after you hit a smash, and note the direction of each move. If your placement is on target but you frequently fail to get to the second shot in time, reduce the smash speed by one level for the next set and compare the results again.
